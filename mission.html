Toucan Music

Our mission is music within reach.

We remove the price and access barriers that keep children outside the music room.

Mission statement

Every kid should get to play something.

Seven of us run this, and we are all Palo Alto teenagers. We teach piano, violin, and viola at Mitchell Park Community Center on weekends. Nobody pays for lessons and nobody pays to perform. We do not ask what a family earns, and we do not ask whether a kid has played before. Wanting to try is the whole requirement.

Free means free

No tuition, no recital fee, no instrument to rent.

You learn in a room with other kids

Practising is easier when you are not doing it alone.

Run by teenagers

Seven of us, plus the families and volunteers who turn up early to set out chairs.
